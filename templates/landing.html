Everything is in your browser. No software to install.

Investigate

Paste a domain, IP, email, username, phone, URL, crypto address or company. The type is detected automatically and lookups run for you.

How it works

  1. Create an account

    Email and password. You accept the rules of use once.

  2. Say why you are looking

    Pick a purpose for each investigation, for example checking your own assets or a suspected scam.

  3. Get results and save them

    Review the findings, follow the suggested next steps and keep what matters in a case.

Rules of use

Public does not mean harmless. These rules are part of the terms you accept.

Good uses

  • Checking your own accounts, domains and assets
  • Security work you are authorised to do
  • Journalism, fact-checking and research in the public interest
  • Checking a suspected scam or phishing attempt

Not allowed

  • Stalking, harassing, threatening or doxxing anyone
  • Finding where a private person lives or works
  • Fraud, blackmail or any unlawful purpose
  • Automated mass scanning of people

Accounts that break these rules can be removed. Read the full Acceptable Use Policy.
