## Not advice

Nothing in OSINT Hub, its results or its documentation is legal, security, investigative, financial or professional advice. Talk to a qualified professional before you act on anything important.

## Results can be wrong

- Results come from public third-party sources that can be wrong, incomplete, outdated, manipulated or about someone or something else with the same name.
- A username that exists on a website is **not proof** that it belongs to a particular person. The same name is often used by different people.
- A domain registration, IP location or phone carrier can be hidden, shared, forged or out of date. IP location is approximate. A phone number's carrier shows only the original allocation.
- Blockchain data shows transactions, not who controls an address.
- If a source is down, slow or blocks the request, that part of a result is missing or marked as unavailable. A missing result is not proof that nothing exists.

Treat every result as a **lead to verify**, never as a conclusion.
