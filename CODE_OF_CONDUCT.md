# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want OSINT Hub to be a welcoming project for everyone who wants to use these skills to protect and help people, whatever their background, country, gender, age, ability or experience.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give and accept feedback kindly and focus on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of insults, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Do not share real people's private information in issues, pull requests, screenshots or examples. Use made-up or clearly public example targets such as `example.com`.

## What we will not accept

- Harassment, threats, hate speech or sexual attention.
- Using the project, its issue tracker or its community to plan or carry out stalking, doxxing, harassment or other harm to people.
- Requests or contributions for face identification of strangers, personal-data scraping, or unauthorised access. See [docs/ETHICS_AND_LEGAL.md](docs/ETHICS_AND_LEGAL.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may block people who keep breaking it. To report a concern, use the private vulnerability-reporting button on the repository's Security tab or contact the maintainers through the contact on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
